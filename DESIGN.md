## Brand & Style

The design system expresses a fusion between prestigious notary authority and tender, ceremonial romance. It reimagines traditional bureaucratic notary procedures ("cartório") into an elevated, emotional milestone for couples, combining timeless editorial gravitas with effortless SaaS ergonomics.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Tone
- **Romantic Editorial Meets Modern Utility:** A synthesis of classic editorial typography, ceremonial parchment textures, and high-precision digital input mechanics.
- **Tone & Mood:** Ceremonial, authentic, affectionate, and luxurious. It balances official validation with warmth and intimacy.
- **Visual Texture:** Subtle tactile cream and parchment undertones, hairline gold borders, and velvety wine-tinted glassmorphic badges.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep burgundy tones against delicate, skin-toned blushes, warm archival parchments, and celebratory golden accents.

### Color Architecture
- **Primary (`#72092C`):** Deep registry wine; commands primary call-to-actions, hero headings, official notary seals, and active application states.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`):** Burnished champagne gold; conveys ceremonial prestige, VIP tags, crest embellishments, and ornamental certificate borders.
- **Tertiary (`#8B1E3F`):** Vivid crimson velvet; utilized for micro-actions, love counters, celebrating badges, and emotive accents.
- **Neutral (`#1C1917`):** Warm graphite ink; guarantees effortless legibility on cream surfaces while steering clear of cold, stark blacks.

### Surface Tones & Canvases
- **Page Foundation:** Tinted parchment white (`#FDFBF7` / `#FCF9F2`) ensures warmth across broad backgrounds.
- **Card & Form Surfaces:** Pure clean milk white (`#FFFFFF`) with whisper-light rose borders (`#EFE5DF`) creates clear distinction between app tools and ceremonial document displays.

## Typography

The type system creates contrast between formal, monumental display elements and ergonomic form inputs.

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys celebratory weight, tradition, and timeless romance across certificate banners, key panel headers, and ceremonial statements.
- **Product & Interface Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern geometric grotesk engineered for legibility across dense form entries, document meta tables, and administrative actions.
- **Notary Sub-headers & Micro-labels:** Displayed in uppercase tracking (+0.06em to +0.08em) using medium-weight `Plus Jakarta Sans` or italicized `Playfair Display` to replicate legal certificate registers.

## Layout & Spacing

The workspace uses a split-screen workbench layout calibrated for desktop live-preview authoring, adapting down into a sequential wizard flow on mobile devices.

### Workspace Grid Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Asymmetric two-column split. The left column (420px–480px fixed width or 5/12 columns) houses the multi-tab form controls, nested steps, and privacy notices. The right fluid panel (7/12 columns) houses the certificate canvas preview anchored within a warm archival viewport.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Collapsible vertical stack with a persistent floating bottom toolbar or dynamic sheet for the live certificate view.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked mode. Sticky header containing document status and step tracker; full-width input container; sticky bottom trigger to toggle live certificate sheet preview.

### Spacing Cadence
Built upon a strict 4px/8px incremental grid. Input form cards utilize `space-md` (16px) internal padding and `space-sm` (8px) element gaps, maintaining a compact editorial layout that prevents unnecessary scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient illumination rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Foundation Layer (Canvas):** Flat parchment tone `#FDFBF7` with optional whisper-light grain.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Form Containers & Panels):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#FDFBF7`, framed by hairline stroke `1px solid #EFE5DF`, paired with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(114, 9, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Preview Certificate):** Elevated parchment document floating over viewport using layered luxury shadows: `0 2px 4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(114, 9, 44, 0.08)`.
- **Glassmorphism & Badges:** VIP and floating notification banners use translucent blush glass: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design incorporates a balanced roundedness philosophy (`0.5rem` / `8px` default corner radius) that merges modern SaaS usability with organic softness.

- **Buttons & Control Inputs:** `rounded-md` (8px) provides a structured feel for inputs while maintaining softness.
- **Badges, Pills & Time Counters:** `rounded-full` (9999px) captures an intimate, jewelry-like feel for VIP tags, date badges, and time stamps.
- **Certificate Archetype:** The live document keeps crisp, micro-rounded corners (`4px` / `rounded-sm`) framed by dual concentric hairline borders (`#C5A059`) to emulate archival paper documents.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Brand Button:** Solid wine background (`#72092C`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), bold typography, subtle wine glow on hover (`0 4px 14px rgba(114, 9, 44, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Action Button:** Warm cream background with subtle wine or gold hairline border (`1px solid #E5D2A8`), tinted hover state (`#FDF8F7`).
- **VIP & Special Actions:** Champagne gold gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F5EEDC 0%, #E5D2A8 100%)`) with dark bronze text (`#5B4210`) and a pill-shaped gold tag.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** Pure white fill, border `1px solid #E5DCD8`, corner radius `8px`, subtle transition.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#72092C` with a soft rose-ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(114, 9, 44, 0.08)`.
- **Field Labels:** Uppercase tracking (`label-md`), color `#57534E`, semibold weight, placed cleanly above input fields.

### Chips & Horizontal Wizard Navigation
- **Step Navigation Chips:** Pill-shaped items along the workflow header. Active step features a rose tint (`#FFF0F3`), wine text (`#72092C`), and icon accent. Inactive steps feature muted graphite (`#57534E`) with transparent or soft ivory borders.
- **Document Selector Tabs:** Compact tab chips allowing rapid switching between *Certidão Oficial*, *Contrato de Amor*, and *RG do Amor*.

### Date & Time Milestone Banners
- **Relationship Counter Card:** Soft blush background (`#FFF0F3`) framed in a rose-gold stroke (`#EFE5DF`), pill-shaped layout, displaying relationship duration in bold wine text (`Playfair Display` or semibold sans).

### Modals & Privacy Drawers
- **Security & Privacy Drawer:** Anchored floating container with shield icon in crimson hue, frosted translucent background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`), clear dual-action confirmation buttons (*Entendido* / *Ler Termos*).
